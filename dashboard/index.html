Method and evidence: what this radar claims, and what backs it

The board ranks every protocol that records no token and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of one that trades, by one size curve fitted on the snapshot’s own traded protocols. Implied value = round(exp(7.9295 + 0.4617 × log(TVL))), least squares on the logs of 118 traded comparables, slope clamped to [0, 1]. The fitted TVL range runs from $5,045,118 to $18,721,539,391; rows outside it are flagged as extrapolated. No protocol name is special-cased and no row is hand-picked.

Blind tests, median absolute log error and share within 2x. Leave-one-out predicts each comparable from a fit that never saw it. Walk-forward fits on the 43 comparables listed before 2024-02-28 and tests on the 44 listed at or after it. A term was kept only when it beat the bare curve on both tests; none did.

Uncertainty, measured three ways. The leave-one-out error quartiles set each row’s range, 0.2618x to 3.0939x, and 51.7% of held-out market caps land inside their own band (n=118). A wider 80% band from the error deciles, 0.1022x to 10x, holds 77.1%. A seeded bootstrap of 200 refits gives a third band per row: slope 5th/median/95th percentile 0.2874 / 0.4557 / 0.6149, intercept 5.3150 / 8.0788 / 11.2054.

Launch odds are a second model, not a second value. A logistic regression on log TVL, 7d growth and age (features standardised, L2 penalty), fitted on all 442 non-product protocols in the snapshot, 244 recording a token and 198 not. Five-fold stratified cross-validation: AUC 0.727, log-loss 0.6096 against 0.6877 for the base rate of 55.2%. Standardised coefficients: intercept 0.2363, log TVL 0.3396, growth -0.5783, age 0.8115. The odds are a descriptive base rate — among protocols this old, this big and growing this fast, this share records a token — never a forecast that a token launches: the snapshot holds no future outcome, and an old protocol that never launched may never launch.

Farm verdicts follow fixed numeric rules, the same for every row: SKIP when TVL is outside the fitted range or 7d growth is at or below -30%; THIN when growth is below -10% or launch odds are below 25%; otherwise FARM. The board carries 131 FARM, 65 THIN and 2 SKIP. Every farm plan has seven dated steps, each with a cost and a time, including a mechanism step that states what activity counts from the category alone. Capital is $50 to $250 by TVL; gas is $12 setup plus $6 a week on Ethereum, $3 plus $1 a week elsewhere; active time is valued at $15 an hour; the exit rule is TVL down 30% or 7d growth below -20% on two weekly checks.

Coverage: 758 protocols scanned, 198 ranked (the whole 40-protocol watchlist plus 158 early rows below the $50M floor), 416 live-token protocols set aside, 144 excluded as products and named in the Model & coverage tab. Snapshot dated 2026-11-14, DefiLlama protocol index.

How to check this radar

  • A working radar. The board covers 40 of 40 watchlist protocols plus every early pre-token row, each with an implied value. Every detail view recomputes its row from TVL and the fitted multiple and shows whether the recompute matches board.json.
  • A value model from the snapshot’s own figures. The curve, the blind tests, the bands and the odds above are all computed from this snapshot by one program, main.ts; the 416 live-token protocols are set aside from the ranking and their ratios are the comparables. Growth and age enter through launch odds, cross-validated, not through the value curve, which the blind tests rejected them from.
  • Farm plans with costs and times. Seven dated steps per row, each with a cost and a time, a mechanism step, a break-even in dollars, an expectation break-even scaled by launch odds, and a numeric exit rule.
  • A page to use. The board sorts and filters by search, category, chain and watchlist; every row opens a detail view; the live-token view and the Model & coverage tab carry the fit, the tests, the odds, the coverage and a curve explorer.

Method and evidence

The board ranks every protocol that records no token by one size curve fitted on the snapshot’s own traded protocols. Implied value = round(exp(7.9295 + 0.4617 × log(TVL))), least squares on the logs of 118 traded comparables, slope clamped to [0, 1]. The fitted TVL range runs from $5,045,118 to $18,721,539,391; rows outside it are flagged as extrapolated. No name is special-cased; no row is hand-picked.

Blind tests, median absolute log error and share within 2x. Leave-one-out predicts each comparable from a fit that never saw it. Walk-forward fits on the 43 comparables listed before 2024-02-28 and tests on the 44 listed at or after it. A term was kept only when it beat the bare curve on both tests; none did.

Uncertainty. The leave-one-out error quartiles set each row’s range, 0.2618x to 3.0939x, and 51.7% of held-out market caps land inside their own band (n=118). A wider 80% band, 0.1022x to 10x, holds 77.1%. A seeded bootstrap of 200 refits gives a third band per row: slope 5th/median/95th percentile 0.2874 / 0.4557 / 0.6149, intercept 5.3150 / 8.0788 / 11.2054.

Launch odds are a second model, not a second value. A logistic regression on log TVL, 7d growth, age and one-hot flags for the six most common categories (features standardised, L2 penalty), fitted on all 442 non-product protocols in the snapshot, 244 recording a token and 198 not. Five-fold stratified cross-validation: AUC 0.7561, log-loss 0.5850 against 0.6877 for the base rate of 55.2%. Standardised coefficients: intercept 0.2074, log TVL 0.4353, growth -0.6419, age 0.7124; Dexs 0.2445, Lending 0.1999, Risk Curators -0.5156, Yield 0.1255, RWA -0.059, Onchain Capital Allocator -0.2363. The shortlist is counted from the data, re-counted per fold, never hand-picked. Category predicts whether a protocol records a token, not what it would be worth: the same terms lost as value terms. Growth and age were tested as value terms in 40+ variants and none survived, so both enter through launch odds instead. The odds are a descriptive base rate — never a forecast that a token launches: the snapshot holds no future outcome.

Verdicts follow fixed rules, the same for every row: SKIP when TVL is outside the fitted range or 7d growth is at or below -30%; THIN when growth is below -10% or launch odds are below 25% or the net at a $1,000 ticket and a 10% farmers' pool is not positive; otherwise FARM. The board carries 7 FARM, 189 THIN and 2 SKIP. Every farm plan has seven dated steps, each with a cost and a time, including a mechanism step. Capital is the $1,000 ticket; gas $12 setup plus $6 a week on Ethereum, $3 plus $1 elsewhere; time at $15 an hour; exit on TVL down 30% or 7d growth below -20% on two weekly checks.

Net economics: a farm pays only when its expected allocation covers its costs. Expected allocation = launch odds × 10% × implied value × ($1,000 / TVL): the 10% farmers' pool is an assumption, split pro-rata by deposit share — the snapshot carries no drop data — and the page re-runs the numbers at 5% or 15%. Costs are the plan's gas plus time at $15/hour plus 4% a year forgone on the $1,000 ticket. At that default, 7 rows pay; the median FARM row of the old rule returned $13.74 expected for $30.93 of cost. The best farm by net is Katana DEX at +$21.88, but it stays THIN: its 7d growth is -10.1%. The largest row, Sentora Curator, never pays at any ticket: its expected allocation per dollar is below the 4% risk-free rate.

Coverage: 758 protocols scanned, 342 audited — 198 ranked (the whole 40-protocol watchlist plus 158 early rows) and 144 excluded as products, named in the Model & coverage tab: CEX 42, Liquid Staking 38, Bridge 28, Canonical Bridge 15, versioned names 11, wrapped/staked by name 5, Liquid Restaking 3, Restaked BTC 2. A further 416 live-token protocols are set aside; snapshot dated 2026-11-14, DefiLlama.

How to check this radar

  • A working radar. The board covers 40 of 40 watchlist protocols plus every early pre-token row, each with an implied value. Every detail view recomputes its row from TVL and the fitted multiple and shows whether the recompute matches board.json.
  • A value model from the snapshot’s own figures. The curve, the blind tests, the bands and the odds above are all computed from this snapshot by one program, main.ts; the 416 live-token protocols are set aside from the ranking and their ratios are the comparables. Growth, age and category were tested as value terms in 40+ variants and rejected by the blind tests, so all three enter through launch odds, cross-validated, not the value curve.
  • Farm plans with costs and times. Seven dated steps per row, each with a cost and a time, a mechanism step, a break-even in dollars, an expectation break-even scaled by launch odds, a net at the stated ticket and pool share, the ticket at which the plan starts to pay, and a numeric exit rule.
  • A page to use. The board sorts and filters by search, category, chain and watchlist; every row opens a detail view; the live-token view and the Model & coverage tab carry the fit, tests, odds, coverage and explorer.
